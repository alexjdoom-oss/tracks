Tracks privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Tracks is a personal app used only by its owner, Alex Doom. When the owner connects a Google account, Tracks reads and writes events on the Google Calendar the owner picks, using the Google Calendar API.

  • What is accessed: calendar events (title, time, location, description) on the chosen calendar.
  • Where it is kept: in a database on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any other server.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tracks' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
